CTR
Click-through rate. Of everyone who saw your ad or listing, the share who clicked. Low CTR means your offer or headline is wrong.
CPC
Cost per click. What Google charges you for one visitor. Set by competition in your trade and your area — you can lower it, not avoid it.
CVR
Conversion rate. Of the visitors who land on your site, the share who call or fill in the form. This is the one your website controls.
CPL
Cost per lead. Budget ÷ leads. The number that tells you whether advertising is sane for your job size.
CAC
Cost to acquire one paying customer. CPL ÷ close rate. Compare it against job value, not against your ad budget.
ROAS
Return on ad spend. Revenue ÷ ad spend. 4× means every dollar in brings four back. Below your margin, you're buying work at a loss.
